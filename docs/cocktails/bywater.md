## Ingredients { #ingredients }

<!-- Decimals are allowed, fractions are not. For ranges, use only a single dash
and no spaces between the numbers. -->
- 1.75 oz aged rum
- 0.75 oz Averna
- 0.5 oz green Chartreuse
- 0.25 oz falernum
- 2 dashes Peychaud's bitters
- 2 dashes orange bitters

## Directions

<!-- If you have a direction that refers to a number of some ingredient, wrap
the number in asterisks and add `{.ingredient-num}` afterwards. For example,
write `Add 2 Tbsp oil to pan` as `Add *2*{.ingredient-num} to pan`. This allows
us to properly change the number when changing the serves value. -->

1. Stir all ingredients with ice, then strain into chilled glass.
2. Twist a piece of orange peel over the drink to express its oils and discard.
3. Garnish with a cherry.

## Notes

<!-- Delete section if no additional notes -->

Originally developed with single aged rum, also works with 1 oz of gentle aged rum and 0.75 oz overproof Jamaican rum.
